## Direction contract

THESIS: The site stops performing "hacker terminal" (glowing HUD panels, scanline brackets, console chrome — the cyberpunk-portfolio default) and instead reads as a Japanese rail departure board: Prentice's roles, projects, and skills are services scheduled and dispatched on a line, each a row with a code, a status, and a destination, legible at a commuter's glance.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black platform ground (#0a0a0c). Primary ink is split-flap amber (#ffb703-family) replacing cyan as the dominant color; cooperative cyan-teal survives only as the "in service / active" status color; vermillion red is reserved for the current/priority row (the PANW role) and alert states, echoing a hanko stamp. Type: "Train One" (Japanese transit-signage display face) for the station-name hero and section headers; "DotGothic16" (genuine Japanese dot-matrix/LED font, full kanji support) for codes, digits, stats, and bilingual labels; "Noto Sans JP" for any kanji/katakana running text; "Rajdhani" retained for body paragraphs (legible long-form). Components: horizontal departure rows (platform-code badge + line name + destination/company + status pill + duration) replace HUD panels; thin double-rule dividers replace glow borders; split-flap character tiles replace plain numerals for GPA/grad-date/stats, with a flip-reveal as the signature interaction; section numerals become platform numbers.

STORY: A visitor reads the hero as a station header (big split-flap name, a "NOW BOARDING" style current-role line), scans a departure-board strip for navigation, scans experience as a timetable of real stops at real companies (not claims), and leaves believing Prentice operates with the same precision as the infrastructure depicted.

No HUD/bracket-cornered panel chrome anywhere in the viewport.

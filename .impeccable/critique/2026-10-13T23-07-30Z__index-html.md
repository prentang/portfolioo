## Overall Impression

Genuinely well-crafted, thematically disciplined portfolio. The real risk is that its most ambitious ideas (scan-to-reveal, the always-on FX stack, the boot sequence) are optimized for "look what I can build" rather than "hire me fast." Biggest opportunity: let Breach Protocol carry the showmanship, and let Experience/Projects be read at a glance with nothing gating them.

## What's Working

1. Thematic coherence all the way through, not just skin-deep — the same bevel/clip-path/HUD vocabulary appears in nav, hero, panels, buttons, and the minigame modal.
2. Accessibility hygiene in the base markup is genuinely good — clean heading hierarchy, meaningful alt text, correct aria-hidden on decorative overlays, prefers-reduced-motion honored comprehensively.
3. Defensive engineering that quietly reinforces the pitch — Lenis CDN script feature-detected with silent fallback; localStorage guarded the same way.

## Priority Issues

[P1] Scan-to-reveal gates the portfolio's strongest differentiator
- Why it matters: Projects are the strongest proof-of-work in a DevOps-leaning portfolio; redacting descriptions behind a hover-triggered decrypt adds friction in front of exactly the content a time-pressured recruiter needs fastest.
- Fix: Show descriptions by default; keep "scan" as decorative flourish, not a gate.
- Suggested command: /impeccable distill

[P1] Always-on ambient FX stack competes with content legibility
- Why it matters: Scanlines, grain, vignette, HUD frame/clock, canvas particles, cursor-ring, parallax, and per-card tilt+glare all run simultaneously (17 separate glow-shadow declarations confirmed by detector). Drives the lowest heuristic score (2/4) and two cognitive-load failures.
- Fix: Cut to the 2-3 effects that read as unmistakably cyberpunk; bump HUD-label/music-caption text (10.2-10.9px) to a comfortable size.
- Suggested command: /impeccable quieter

[P2] Music player disappears at common laptop widths
- Why it matters: `.music-player { left: calc(48.95vw + 539px); right: 28px; }` with no explicit width computes to negative/near-zero width across roughly 921-1110px viewports. Reproducible bug, not a judgment call.
- Fix: Give it an explicit min-width or clamp()-based width.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

[P2] "About" nav item doesn't reach the About content
- Why it matters: href="#about" lands on the hero section, not the bio/education panel in the separate #about-detail section. Reads as a dead/broken link.
- Fix: Move id="about" onto the bio section.
- Suggested command: /impeccable audit

[P2] Boot sequence delays primary CTAs on every visit
- Why it matters: Locks the page and types for up to 4000ms on every load, with no visible skip cue and no memory of prior visits.
- Fix: Add a visible skip affordance and/or skip automatically after first visit via localStorage.
- Suggested command: /impeccable onboard

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prentice Tang's professional network: recruiters and hiring managers screening him for SWE/DevOps/security internship and new-grad roles, plus professors, peers, and people he meets at conferences/meetups. It is a general professional presence, not a narrowly single-purpose job-hunt funnel — visitors arrive to evaluate or simply learn about him, then decide whether to reach out, refer, or connect.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio/resume site that represents Prentice Tang (CS student, graduating Dec 2026) to anyone evaluating him professionally. Success means a visitor quickly forms an accurate, credible picture of his real experience and projects, and has an easy path to his resume or to contact him.

## Positioning

What differentiates this portfolio from a typical CS student's is real production experience, not class projects: Prentice has already worked inside real companies (Palo Alto Networks, Sonos, CyberArk, Draeger). The site should lean on that track record as its credibility anchor — the resume/experience content carries the weight, with the design supporting and surfacing it rather than competing with it for attention.

## Operating Context

Static site, no backend. Content (experience, projects, leadership, GPA, graduation date) is sourced from Prentice's resume PDF, which he updates periodically and the site is then synced to match. Deployed via GitHub Pages at prentang.github.io.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No phone number may ever appear anywhere on the site or in the resume PDF — a standing hard constraint, checked after every resume sync.
- No fabricated claims, metrics, testimonials, or evidence may be added — all experience/project content must trace to the real resume.
- Resume PDF (`Prentice_Tang_Resume.pdf`) is the single source of truth for experience copy; site content is synced to it, not the reverse.

## Brand Commitments

Cyberpunk visual theme is an explicit, standing brief for this site and should be preserved across redesigns (not treated as incumbent-system inertia to discard). Current redesign request adds Japanese aesthetic tones/coloring within that cyberpunk world (evocative of Japanese-cyberpunk visual culture — neon Tokyo, not a generic Western cyberpunk palette).

## Evidence on Hand

Real experience: DevOps/Security Engineer Intern at Palo Alto Networks (current), prior roles at Sonos, CyberArk, Draeger. GPA 3.72/4.00, graduating Dec 2026. Real projects include a Breach Protocol minigame and netrunner-themed project cards already built into the site. Resume PDF is present in both repos and kept current.

## Product Principles

1. Real experience is the credibility anchor — design should surface and dignify it, never bury or outshine it with decoration.
2. The cyberpunk theme is a committed brand identity, not a default to abandon; evolve it (e.g., toward Japanese-cyberpunk tones), don't replace it with something unrelated.
3. Content integrity is non-negotiable: no invented claims, no phone number, resume PDF stays source of truth.
4. The site serves a broad professional audience, not only recruiters — it should read as a credible general presence, not a narrow pitch deck.
5. Visual ambition is welcome, but must stay legible and scannable for a visitor deciding quickly whether to read further or reach out.
